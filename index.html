From breathtaking mountain landscapes to ancient rice terraces, the Cordillera Administrative Region offers a beautiful blend of nature, history, and living cultural traditions.

Explore the Highlands

About the Cordillera Region

The Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR) is located in Northern Luzon, Philippines. It is known for its cool highlands, mountain scenery, traditional weaving, and remarkable cultural heritage. CAR includes Abra, Apayao, Benguet, Ifugao, Kalinga, Mountain Province, and the highly urbanized city of Baguio.

Beautiful Tourist Attractions

Banaue Rice Terraces

These remarkable terraces reflect generations of Ifugao knowledge and agricultural traditions, surrounded by beautiful mountain scenery.

Mount Pulag

A popular mountain destination known for its highland landscapes, sunrise views, and sea of clouds.

Baguio City

Known as the Summer Capital of the Philippines, Baguio features parks, markets, gardens, and a cool climate.

Sagada

A peaceful mountain destination known for its caves, scenic views, and distinctive cultural heritage.

Kaparkan Falls

Located in Abra, this natural attraction is admired for its cascading waters and distinctive limestone formations.

Food and Culture

Pinikpikan

A traditional Cordilleran dish associated with cultural practices and community gatherings.

Strawberries

Baguio and nearby highland areas are popular for fresh strawberries and strawberry-based treats.

Traditional Weaving

Travel Guide

Best Time to Visit

The dry season is generally suitable for outdoor trips. Check weather conditions before visiting mountain areas.

Transportation

Baguio is accessible by bus from Metro Manila. Other destinations may require local buses or vans.

What to Bring

Prepare warm clothing, comfortable footwear, drinking water, and rain protection for changing mountain weather.

Responsible Travel

Respect local customs, follow community guidelines, and leave natural places clean.

Where to Stay

Hotels

Hotels in major destinations offer convenient access to restaurants, shops, and attractions.

Guesthouses

Guesthouses and homestays can offer a welcoming way to experience local hospitality.

Mountain Lodging

Some highland destinations have simple accommodations near hiking trails and nature spots.

Festivals and Events

Panagbenga Festival

Baguio's Flower Festival celebrates flowers, creativity, and the culture of the city.

Lang-ay Festival

A cultural celebration in Mountain Province that showcases local heritage and traditions.

Community Celebrations

Various communities celebrate their distinct customs, cultural practices, and local heritage.
